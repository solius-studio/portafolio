# Trust Engineering & Authority Architecture

## 1. The Trust Dilemma in Agency Sales
High-value clients (law firms, local commerce owners, e-commerce founders) hesitate to purchase custom web and AI solutions due to 4 core anxieties:
1. **Fear of Technical Failure**: *"Will this bot hallucinate or break down during peak hours?"*
2. **Fear of Vendor Lock-In**: *"If I hire them, will I be trapped forever in proprietary software?"*
3. **Fear of Hidden Costs**: *"Will maintenance and API tokens cost an unexpected fortune?"*
4. **Fear of Invisible Value**: *"How do I know this is actually better than a $50 WordPress template?"*

Every page in Solius Studio must actively and tangibly dismantle these 4 fears.

### B. The "Zero Vendor Lock-In" Guarantee Badge
Display clear contractual assurances:
- **Propiedad Total del Código**: El cliente recibe el repositorio GitHub y la propiedad absoluta de sus activos.
- **Sin Cuotas Obligatorias**: Sin cuotas forzadas de mantenimiento. Si el cliente quiere gestionar su web, puede hacerlo.
- **Despliegue en Cuentas Propias**: Vercel, Cloudflare Pages o AWS a nombre del cliente.

### C. Before vs. After Tangible Comparison Table
Contrast traditional legacy web development against Solius Studio engineering:
- *Antiguo (WordPress / Plugins genéricos)*: Carga lenta (4.5s), plugins vulnerables, costos recurrentes de hosting lento.
- *Solius Studio (Astro + CDN Edge + IA)*: Instantánea (0.2s), arquitectura estática invulnerable a SQL injection, costo de hosting $0 en capas edge gratuitas.

## 3. Transparency & Direct Founder Access
- **Direct Engineer Channel**: Provide a direct, friction-free WhatsApp chat link with the engineering founders.
- **Step-by-Step Delivery Timeline**: Never leave delivery dates ambiguous. Every proposal and process section must specify exact milestones (ej: Prototipo funcional en 72hs, Entrega final en 7 a 10 días).
- **Data Protection & Privacy**: Explicitly guarantee that client business data and customer leads are never used to train public LLM models.
